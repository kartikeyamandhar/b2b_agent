# Adjudication eval pre-registration (M1)

Committed before the rules-only adjudicator is run on the golden claims. Changes after the first run need a decisions-log entry and are reported as a separate row.

## Golden claims

- Source: the 20 synth-source golden notices (evals/extraction/PREREG.md), using their gold rules, not extracted rules, so extraction errors cannot leak into adjudication numbers.
- 15 claims per notice, 300 total, generated by `python -m recallops.synth --golden` with evals/adjudication/planted.yaml (seed 42). The mix was set once before any adjudicator ran: duplicate weights were lowered from 0.6 to 0.25 because the first draw flagged 52 percent of claims; nothing else was changed.
- Expected outcomes come from evals/adjudication/outcome_policy.yaml (draft pending the owner's sign-off) with precedence flag > deny > needs_info > approve. A planted duplicate flags both claims in the pair.
- Composition of the frozen set: 102 approve, 87 deny, 72 flag, 39 needs_info; 43 claims with two violations; image tiers 179 clean, 85 mild, 36 hard.
- Adjudicators see golden_claims.json only. Label sidecars under labels/ are read only by the scorer (recallops.evals.adjudication); nothing under apps/api/src/recallops/claims reads them, and a test enforces that.
- M2 adds 60 owner-reviewed claims and 40 classmate-written adversarial claims, reported as separate blocks.

## Metrics

- outcome accuracy: share of claims whose predicted outcome equals the expected outcome (4 classes).
- macro F1: unweighted mean of per-class F1 over approve, deny, needs_info, flag. This is the number the CLAUDE.md kill criterion compares (rules-only within 0.05 of the agent), on the point estimate.
- approval accuracy: share of claims where (predicted approve) equals (expected approve).
- fraud precision and recall: positive class is flag.
- needs_info precision.
- share routed to human: flagged, or approved with refund above the program value threshold (10,000 cents by default).
- mean steps, mean cost USD, p50 latency (seconds per claim).
- Per-violation recall: for claims carrying a violation, the share whose predicted outcome equals the expected outcome; destruction_not_shown is also split by subtype (missing, label_reupload, intact).

## Expectations stated before the run

- The rules-only adjudicator has no vision, so destruction_not_shown with subtype intact cannot be caught in M1 (expected recall 0 on that subtype); missing and label_reupload should be caught.
- Duplicate detection uses sha256 and a 6-bit pHash threshold; near-duplicate photo copies are planted only when their pHash distance is 6 or less, so planted photo duplicates should be caught.
- The kill criterion cannot be evaluated in M1: there is no agent row yet. adjudication.md says so in its header.
